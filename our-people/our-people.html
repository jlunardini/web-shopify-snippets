Mike Volchok

President

Mike started in 1993 with a pair of rollerblades and a belief that IT support could be both knowledgable and kind. From his start on his own, he built a consulting business that supported hundreds of clients. In 2007 he opened Mike's Tech Shop, Chelsea's Nice Little Friendly Mac shop. Finally, Mike launched MTS Pro Services to provide Managed IT Services to small and medium businesses. While Mike's name is on the door, the credit of all MTS's success goes mostly to the incredible team of people who deliver the services every day.
